Component architecture

Component view

Five zones, two enforcement points, one policy decision. Requests enter through the Web Proxy or the SSH Certificate Authority and never reach a resource without a decision.

Passkey login sequence

Sequence diagram

A WebAuthn ceremony, a risk decision, and a server-side session cookie. No password is transmitted and no biometric leaves the device.

SSH certificate issuance

Sequence diagram

The SSH helper proves possession, requests a thirty-minute certificate for one principal and one host, and opens the SPA port only long enough to connect.

Just-in-time approval

Sequence diagram

A step-up score becomes a human decision. The approver records a reason; the grant is capped at thirty minutes and the queue waits fifteen before it expires.

Mid-session kill

Sequence diagram

A posture change drops the score below forty. The Policy Administrator kills the session at both enforcement points and revokes the outstanding certificate.

Policy Engine decision logic

Activity diagram

Hard gates first, then rules plus the ML adjustment. The score selects one of four bands; a failed hard gate overrides the score and fails closed.

Session lifecycle

State machine

Active, step-up, suspended, killed, and expired. Suspended waits for approval, step-up requires re-authentication, and killed and expired are terminal.

NIST SP 800‑207 · ZeroGate v0.9

JIT request lifecycle

State machine

Requested, pending, approved, denied, expired, and consumed. An approval is single-use and expires after thirty minutes if it is never consumed.

NIST SP 800‑207 · ZeroGate v0.9

Device trust lifecycle

State machine

Unknown, enrolled, compliant, non-compliant, quarantined, and revoked. A quarantined device re-enrolls only after the posture finding is remediated.
